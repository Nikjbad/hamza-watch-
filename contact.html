Before you order

The useful answers.

How quickly do you ship?

Orders typically leave within 48 hours and arrive in 3–6 business days across India.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

Yes, COD is available on most pincodes. We confirm eligibility with you on WhatsApp.

What does the warranty cover?

Two years for the movement and case. Straps and glass have six months of manufacturing-defect cover.

How do returns work?

Unworn watches in original packaging can be returned within 14 days of delivery. Message us to begin.
